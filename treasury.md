| **Amount** | 270,000,003,476 STER (14.64% of supply) |
| **Created** | August 28, 2026 |
| **Unlocks** | November 27, 2028, three years after the token launch. The full amount unlocks on that one date. |
| **Recipient** | Founders wallet, `ByU6iuYR3rCVtrp1pb9szxTeq6BmSH3pPnhYZ9V7ygMV` |
| **Can it be canceled?** | No. The recipient cannot be changed either. |

While locked, these tokens sit in a Jupiter Lock escrow account, not in the Founders wallet itself. The lock can be found at https://lock.jup.ag/ by searching for the Founders wallet address.

## 🔹 Other Facts About These Wallets

- The Master wallet created the STER token. It is listed as the metadata update authority, but the metadata is immutable, so that authority cannot change anything.
- The Treasury wallet funded Airdrop #1 (see [airdrops.md](airdrops.md)) and is the recipient of two of SDV's liquidity locks (see [lp.md](lp.md)).
- The Treasury, Marketing, and Advisors wallets are not locked. SDV uses them for operating purposes such as community rewards, promotions, and advisor compensation.

A written treasury policy covering each wallet's purpose, limits on movements, and regular public reporting is in preparation. It will be published in this repository when it is adopted.
